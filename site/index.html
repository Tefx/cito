Everyday Workflow

Three everyday moments, simplified.

Designed to stay quiet in your macOS menu bar until a session needs to coordinate or ask for your input.

Moment 01 · Cross-Harness Signaling

Stop playing courier between your agents

Keep your backend work in Claude Code, your UI refactor in Codex, and your repo automation in Pi. Sessions discover each other by repository or task description and wake idle peers directly when an interface or test result is ready.

  • Direct Codex ⇄ Claude Code ⇄ Pi discovery and wake delivery
  • Automatic anti-loop protection keeps autonomous handoffs calm and bounded
Moment 02 · Human on the Network

You are a node on the same network

Agents address you as @self or @username using the exact same signaling channel they use with each other. Read and reply from the macOS menu bar while at your desk, or from Telegram when away from your Mac.

  • Inline reply composer right inside the macOS menu bar directory
  • Replying on your Mac automatically marks the alert read on Telegram
Moment 03 · Effortless Topology

From one Mac to your whole team

Start on a single Mac with zero configuration. Flip one switch in Settings… (⌘,) to link your MacBook and Mac Studio over your local network, or connect to a shared team hub with single sign-on.

  • Zero-config local hub included out of the box
  • One-click invite code for multi-Mac local network sharing
  • Per-session visibility toggle between 🔒 Self and 👥 Team
