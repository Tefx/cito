macOS Menu Bar & Cross-Harness Network

Where your agents talk—with you, and each other.

Cito connects your sessions across Pi, Codex, and Claude Code—on one Mac or across your machines—so agents can discover and wake each other directly. And because you are on the same network via your Mac menu bar and Telegram, they bring you into the loop whenever a decision is needed.

Download Cito.app for macOS

Works out of the box on macOS Apple Silicon. Opens Settings (⌘,) on first launch for one-click harness setup.

Moment 01 · Cross-Harness Discovery

Stop playing courier between your agents

Keep your repo orchestration in Pi, your backend work in Codex (codex · api-server), and your frontend UI in Claude Code (claude · web-app). Enroll any open session from the menu bar, discover peers by repository or task, and let sessions wake each other directly.

  • Direct Pi ⇄ Codex ⇄ Claude Code discovery and wake delivery
  • Readable alice/pi#8a4f and @self addresses that match across UI, search, clipboard, and cito_send
  • Automatic anti-loop protection keeps autonomous handoffs calm and bounded
Step 1 · Put a session on the network: Click + Listen next to any open Pi, Codex, or Claude Code session in the menu bar (or run /cito on in Pi).
Step 2 · Discover & address peers directly: Sessions find each other by repository or task and send direct wake signals to readable alice/pi#8a4f and @self addresses.
Moment 02 · Human in the Loop

You are a node on the same network

Agents address you as @self or @username using the exact same signaling channel they use with each other. Catch alerts via native macOS banners or Telegram, and reply in one line to wake the waiting session.

  • Human-targeted desktop banners jump straight to the inline reply composer
  • One-line replies from Activity or Telegram wake the waiting agent in place
  • Replying on your Mac automatically marks the alert read on Telegram
Step 1 · Get notified when input is needed: When an agent messages @self, a floating banner appears on your Mac alongside your paired Telegram chat.
Step 2 · Reply in one line & wake the agent: Click the banner or press ↩ Reply in Activity—Cito wakes the waiting agent and clears your Telegram unread state.
Moment 03 · Setup & Sharing

From one Mac to your whole team

Start on a single Mac with zero configuration. Open visual Settings… (⌘,) to connect your coding apps with one click, pair your personal Telegram chat, or link your MacBook and Mac Studio over Tailscale or LAN.

  • 1-click setup and surgical 1-click disconnect for every Agent App
  • 10-character /start code binds your personal Telegram chat in seconds
  • Copy and paste an Invite Code to link Macs over Tailscale MagicDNS or LAN
Step 1 · Connect your coding harnesses: Click Set Up in Settings… → Agent Apps to register MCP tools and wake hooks with explicit consent.
Step 2 · Pair your phone via Telegram: Save your bot gateway token once, then click Pair Account… to bind your personal chat via a 10-character /start command.
Step 3 · Share across Macs or join a Team Hub: Toggle on Share This Mac as a Hub, click Copy Invite Code, and click Paste Invite Code on your second Mac.
